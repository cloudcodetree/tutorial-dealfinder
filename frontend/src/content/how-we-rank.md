# How we rank

DealFinder tells you whether a price is a deal. Here is exactly how, as it runs today.

## One signal: our fair price

You enter a product and the price you see. We compare that price with our price model's fair price for that product.

- **Deal**: 15% or more below our fair price.
- **Fair**: within 15% of our fair price.
- **Overpriced**: 15% or more above our fair price.
- **Suspicious**: below 30% of our fair price. That is too cheap to be real, so it may be used, an accessory, or a mislisting.

## Where the fair price comes from

The model is trained per product category on a fixed July 2026 snapshot of store prices. It does not see today's prices, so a fair price can be out of date. Check the store before you buy.

If the model cannot place your product in a category it knows, you get no verdict. We say so instead of guessing.

## What we never do

- The seller's "was" price is never used. A struck-through regular price says nothing about what the product is worth.
- There is no paid placement and there are no affiliate links. Nobody pays us to change a verdict.

## What is not here yet

There are no live store results yet. DealFinder will show them once a store's data is available under terms that allow it.
