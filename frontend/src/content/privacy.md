# Privacy

## Effective date

This policy takes effect on November 2, 2026.

## What we collect

Checking a price needs no account, and DealFinder has no sign-in yet. We store nothing about you.

- **What you type.** The product name and price you enter go to our server, which works out the verdict and answers. They are never stored or logged.
- **Our request log.** For each request we log the page's route (such as `/check`), the answer's status code, how long it took and what it cost us. It holds no query text, no IP address and no browser details.
- **Your IP address.** Our server holds it in memory only, to limit how fast one visitor can send requests, and it is gone when that server instance stops. DealFinder never logs it. Cloudflare sees it when it carries your request.
- **Error reports.** When something breaks, we send an error report to Sentry. It holds the error, the page address without its query, and basic browser details. It holds nothing you typed, no cookies and no IP address from us.

## Who processes it

- **Google Cloud** runs our server and keeps our request log.
- **Cloudflare** serves the site, sits in front of our server, and runs Workers AI for the lab's Ask mode. Cloudflare says Workers AI does not train on what you send it, and keeps nothing after the request.
- **Supabase** hosts our database. It holds no data about visitors today. Its sign-in service is shared with cloudcodetree.com.
- **Sentry** receives the error reports described above.

## Reviewing and changing your data

DealFinder holds no data about you, so there is nothing to review or change. When accounts arrive, the site will let you download everything we hold about you and delete it, and this page will say how.

## Deleting your sign-in account

DealFinder has no sign-in of its own. If you have an account on cloudcodetree.com, it is shared with DealFinder's future sign-in, and deleting DealFinder data will never delete it. To delete that account, use the contact form below.

## Do Not Track

We do not track you across sites, so your browser's Do Not Track setting changes nothing here.

## Tracking by other parties

None. There are no ads and no analytics, and the pages load no scripts from other companies.

## Cookies and storage

DealFinder sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser. Cloudflare may set a security cookie, `__cf_bm` or `cf_clearance`, to tell people from bots.

## How long we keep it

- Our request log: 30 days.
- Error reports: 30 days in Sentry.
- What you type and your IP address: not kept.

## Children

DealFinder is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect anything from them.

## Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, the effective date at the top of this page changes with it.
