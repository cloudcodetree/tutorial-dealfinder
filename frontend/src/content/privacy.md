# Privacy

## Draft: final policy lands before launch

This page states only what is true today.

## Checking a price

Checking a price needs no account. The product name and price you enter go to our server to work out the verdict. We do not save them in a database, and we do not connect them to you.

Our hosting provider's request logs record the address of each request and your browser's user agent, which names your browser and its version. For a check, that address includes the product name and price you entered. Those logs are kept for 30 days. They do not include your IP address.

## Cookies and tracking

This site sets no cookies. It runs no analytics and loads nothing from other companies.
